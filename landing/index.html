THE PROBLEM • UNGROUNDED AI

Scientific Research Deserves Better Than AI Guesswork.

In healthcare and biotechnology, hallucinated citations and unverified claims are dangerous. See how EvidenceBot differs from conventional LLM chatbots.

Conventional AI Chatbots

Prone to Hallucinations & Fabricated Citations

  • Produces plausible-sounding but completely unsupported scientific assertions.
  • Fabricates non-existent PubMed IDs (PMIDs), fake author rosters, and invent paper titles.
  • Routinely confuses observational correlation with direct clinical causality.
  • Answers confidently even when scientific consensus is absent or studies conflict.
  • Operates as a black box without transparent, traceable source evidence.
EvidenceBot Architecture

Grounded Literature Synthesis & Verified PMIDs

  • Retrieves authentic biomedical literature from NCBI PubMed before drafting answers.
  • Employs dense vector embeddings to mathematically extract relevant passages.
  • Strictly constrains answer generation to retrieved peer-reviewed study excerpts.
  • Provides direct, clickable links to original PubMed records for every cited claim.
  • Enforces empirical abstention: transparently refuses to guess when research is lacking.
EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

From Research Question to Scientific Evidence

An end-to-end 4-stage pipeline that guarantees verifiable provenance.

Stage 01

Live PubMed Retrieval

Connects to the NCBI PubMed E-Utilities API suite (esearch & efetch) to search biomedical literature and retrieve available peer-reviewed abstracts.

NCBI E-UTILITIES / XML
Stage 02

Dense Vector Embeddings

Sentence Transformers converts research passages and user queries into 384-dimensional numerical representations that capture biomedical semantics.

ALL-MINILM-L6-V2 / 384-D
Stage 03

FAISS Similarity Search

FAISS compares normalized vector embeddings to rapidly identify and rank research passages that are semantically relevant to the user's inquiry.

FAISS / COSINE SIMILARITY
Stage 04

Evidence-Grounded Responses

An optional OpenAI or Gemini model generates responses using strictly retrieved evidence, with direct references to corresponding PubMed records.
